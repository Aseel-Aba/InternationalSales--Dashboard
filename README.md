# InternationalSales-Dashboard
The  “A Market “  want to create a report for the sales of the following  years  :2023-2024-2025 . So that, the owner of the "A Market “ can understand their customers and grow more sales in 2026 .
## Project Description :
## Page 1 : Report Introduction 
## Page 2 :Project KPI 
Calculating Total Sales , Total Cost , Total Quantity , Total Target , profit , Target Achievement .
## Page 3 : Home 
Using Q&A visual To answer the questions and displayed using charts :

-TOP 10 Product Sub Category in bar chart .

-Percent Of Total Sales by Territory group in pie chart.

-Total Sales by Month in line chart.

-Total Sales by Territory in tree map.

-Total Sales and Total Cost and Total quantity and Profit and Target in new Card.
## Page 4: Legend 
A Line chart showing the total sales for each month of the year ( 2023-2024-2025) using Legend.
## Page 5 : SmallMuli and Card
-A line chart showing total sales for each continent ) Europe – North America- Pacific ) every month over the years using Small Multiples.

-A card showing the top product that made a profit .

- A card showing the product with the lowest profit.
## Page 6 : Product Search 
Searching for the product using the input slicer and displaying its information in a table .
## page 7 : Gauge and KPI
A page that shows whether sales hit the target or not for each month of the year using   Gaudge And KPI visual .
## page 8 : Tooltip visual
Using the tooltip visual , I added extra information to the line chart.
